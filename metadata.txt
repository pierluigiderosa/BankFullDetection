description=Automatic bankfull level and width detection from DTM cross-sections
about=Detects the bankfull level of river cross-sections sampled from a DTM with the
    maximum hydraulic depth method (A/W) described in: De Rosa, P., Fredduzzi, A.,
    Cencetti, C. (2019). A GIS-based tool for automatic bankfull detection from airborne
    high resolution DEM. ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information, 8(11), 480.
    https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi8110480. The A/W curve is smoothed with a cross-validated smoothing
    spline in pure Python (numpy/scipy): R and rpy2 are no longer needed. The optional
    "thalweg" handling of multiple wet polygons follows an idea of HydXS (Garber and
    Thompson 2024, Water Resources Research, doi 10.1029/2023WR035269).
    The tools are available as Processing algorithms.
